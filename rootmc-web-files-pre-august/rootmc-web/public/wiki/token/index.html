Short version: G = gold-backed Minecraft server currency (in-game only). $ROOTMC = speculative Solana memecoin on pump.fun. No exchange between them. No token-gated gameplay. You can lose 100% of any money put into $ROOTMC. Nothing here is investment advice.

Our commitment to compliance

RootMC operates a public Minecraft Java server subject to the Minecraft End User License Agreement (EULA), Commercial Usage Guidelines, and Minecraft Usage Guidelines published by Mojang / Microsoft. We take those rules seriously and design every $ROOTMC-related decision around them.

We are dedicated to remaining Mojang-compliant. That means:

  • Gold standard forever — G is and will remain the only in-game currency. The closed-loop treasury, shops, Towny costs, and loans all run on G — not crypto.
  • No blockchain in the server — we will not ship plugins or server software that read wallets, verify token balances, or connect gameplay to Solana.
  • Fairness for every player — nobody gets survival advantages, exclusive areas, better economy terms, or command perks because they hold $ROOTMC.
  • Written policy first — any future community program involving holders will be documented on this wiki before it goes live, with a clear Mojang-compliance review.
  • Holder expectations — token “utility” is community recognition, transparency, and optional off-game perks — not in-game power or G airdrops.

If Mojang or Microsoft updates their guidelines, we will update this page and adjust our practices to stay within them — even if that means reducing how $ROOTMC is referenced on official channels.

Mojang rules that apply to $ROOTMC

Microsoft has stated that blockchain technologies are not permitted inside Minecraft client and server applications, and that Minecraft in-game content must not be used to create scarce digital assets (including NFT-style exclusivity). The EULA also prohibits creating scarcity of in-game content based on out-of-game conditions — for example, checking whether a player owns a token or NFT to unlock skins, commands, or areas.

RootMC interprets those rules as follows for our server:

What we may do (Mojang-aligned)

These are the kinds of holder / tokenomics ideas consistent with our compliance commitment. None of them replace or bridge into G:

  • Transparency — public notes that creator fees may support hosting, maps, or community events (no guaranteed returns to holders).
  • Off-game recognition — optional “supporter” badge on a linked web profile or cosmetic Discord role, with no gameplay effect.
  • Community governance (Discord only) — polls on meta topics (event themes, build contests, emoji) — not shop taxes, loan limits, dividend rates, or claim costs.
  • Events & prizes — build contests judged off-game; prizes paid in G from staff/treasury budgets, not token airdrops.
  • Education — this wiki, disclaimers, and clear “G ≠ $ROOTMC” messaging in Discord.
  • Implicit community benefit — a healthier server funded by optional community support; not a contractual utility for token holders.

Risks

Memecoins are among the highest-risk assets in crypto. You should assume:

  • Total loss — price can go to zero quickly.
  • Extreme volatility — large up and down moves in minutes are normal.
  • Low liquidity — you may not be able to sell at the price you see on a chart.
  • Smart-contract & platform risk — bugs, exploits, or platform policy changes can affect trading.
  • No deposit insurance — unlike bank balances, on-chain assets are not FDIC/SIPC protected.
  • Tax obligations — trading may be taxable where you live; we do not provide tax guidance.

Only use money you can afford to lose entirely. If you are unsure, do not buy.

How creator revenue is used (server hosting, events, personal expense, etc.) is at the operator’s discretion unless a separate public commitment is made. Creator income does not entitle token holders to refunds, profit sharing, or governance over the Minecraft server.
