Preamble

This document is version 2026-07-06, ratified on July 6, 2026 (HST) via Council poll 549cf16c. It supersedes draft 2026-07-02 (declared July 2, 2026). Further policy changes use the weighted legislature pipeline.

RootMC operates a survival multiplayer economy where G (Gold) is the sole in-game currency. This Constitution defines how gold enters circulation, how the Server Reserve collects and redistributes it, and how staff and automated systems may pay players.

G is not $ROOTMC. In-game Gold is separate from the optional Solana memecoin. See the token disclaimer.

When this document and live server config disagree, staff publish updates here first. Config files (root-essentials.yml, rootmc.yml, root-upkeep.yml, root-ranks.yml, root-loans.yml, Towny) implement these rules on the server.

Foundational principles

  • No money faucet — new players start at 0 G. Gold enters when players mint ore, sell items, trade with each other, or receive treasury redistribution.
  • Closed-loop treasury — the Server Reserve (towny-server) collects taxes, fees, and Towny sinks, then pays dividends, grants, loans, votes, and milestones. It does not print gold from nothing.
  • Gold-backed balances — wallet G represents a claim on physical gold at the fixed mint peg (see Gold redemption).
  • Fair shops — player listing prices are capped at 110% of the rolling market average when an average exists.
  • No server shop — all item purchases come from player chest shops.

Governing body & reserve positives

The RootMC governing body (staff and designated leadership) administers the Server Reserve. When the reserve runs a surplus (positive balance or net inflows), the governing body decides how to use those positives — staff grants, loan capacity, vote pools, playtime milestones, events, and other approved outflows.

Discretionary policy sets timing and priority as long as outflows debit the treasury ledger and follow this Constitution. Reserve negatives are permitted — outflows still debit the ledger; inflows refill over time rather than minting.

Players audit reserve activity with /reserve, /baltop, and the web economy page (Server Reserve section). Community input on reserve use and server policy uses governance voting power (playtime-weighted, listing vote points).

Citizen legislature & governance voting

RootMC runs a web-first citizen legislature. Full proposal and bill text lives on rootmc.net/council. Discord #proposals posts links and discussion threads; #voting hosts weighted Council polls. All voters must accept the Terms of Service and verify a linked Minecraft + Discord account at rootmc.net/verify.

Pipeline

  1. Submit — eligible linked players post constitution, governance, plugin, or metric changes on the site or /proposals submit. A public Discord thread opens for discussion.
  2. Discuss — thread replies are summarized by Grok into neutral bill text before compilation.
  3. Weekly bill — each Sunday (HST), pending proposals that have been published at least 3 days compile into one bill with a 48h amendment window on the site. Late-week submissions (e.g. Saturday) roll to the following week.
  4. Council vote — weighted For / Against / Abstain in #voting (buttons) and on the site. Metrics show weighted % and voter count.

Eligibility to submit or vote

  • Linked Minecraft account + Discord (verified online).
  • ≥ 1 hour total RootMC playtime (check /vote in Discord or in-game).
  • Terms accepted at rootmc.net/terms (current version).

What determines your voting share

Each eligible voter holds a share of 100% of total governance power — all shares sum to exactly 100%. Outcomes use weighted tallies (not one account = one vote).

raw = playtime_seconds × vote_points share = raw ÷ Σ(all players' raw) → your % of 100%

FactorSourceRole
PlaytimeRootMC total playtime (seconds online)Participation weight

Listing vote points

Without any verified listing votes, vote_points = 1 (baseline). Each confirmed listing vote (Votifier callback) adds one vote point to your all-time total — there is no cap. Votifier service names are normalized (e.g. mcsl → Minecraft Server List, minecraftserverslist → MinecraftList.org). Eight official sites are listed in root-rewards.yml; voting the same site again on a later day still counts.

Separate from vote Gold rewards

Listing sites also pay 1–20 G per confirmed vote to your wallet from the Server Reserve. That payout is independent of governance power. Net worth does not affect Council weight.

Vote requirements

Full live summary also on rootmc.net/council.

Weekly bill vote

  • Proposal published ≥ 3 days before Sunday (HST) compile
  • 48h amendment window after the bill posts
  • Council vote runs 7 days — For / Against / Abstain
  • Leading option needs >50% of cast weighted votes

Treasury grant vote

  • Eligible linked players may request 1–10,000 G from reserve at /governance/grant/
  • Proposer cannot vote on their own grant
  • Pass or veto: weighted For > Against (Abstain does not break ties)
  • That majority must hold 24 hours unchanged — then auto-payout or veto
  • Poll may stay open up to 30 days

Weighted tallies

  • Each ballot uses your current share % as vote weight
  • Site and Discord #voting buttons count the same weight
  • You may change your vote until the poll closes (grant clock resets if majority direction flips)

Map return grant (ended)

Program closed July 2026. The one-time 1000 G map return grant and /cmdtest onboarding rewards are disabled in live config. Historical rules below apply only to players who already claimed or were pre-marked at map launch.

After the Paper 26.2 map launch, returning players could claim a one-time 1000 G grant from the Server Reserve while the program was active.

Eligibility (historical)

  • First joined RootMC before 1 July 2026 (HST) — any amount of playtime qualified; no minimum hours.
  • Discord linked — /link in-game, then rootmc.net/verify.
  • One claim per account — tracked server-side.
  • Town resettlement — players who already received the equivalent 1000 G at map launch were marked pre-claimed.

How it worked

  1. /link (or /rootmc link) → complete Discord verification.
  2. /rootmc claim-return (alias /rootstat claim-return) while enabled.

Gold debited the Server Reserve ledger (map-return-grant). New players who first joined on or after 1 July 2026 (HST) were never eligible.

Gold-backed Notes

Wallet G and Reserve Gold (towny-server vault) together form circulating Notes — digital claims backed by physical gold at the mint peg.

  • Backing — post-reset /mint gross (physical gold → wallet G) plus Reserve Gold in the vault.
  • Circulating Notes — player wallet G + Reserve Gold (not town/nation banks).
  • Over-issue — when treasury grants or opening carryover issued Notes without matching /mint, the economy tracks unbacked debt. While materially over-issued, a dynamic transaction tax burns unbacked Notes on taxed activity instead of crediting the reserve.
  • Fully backed — when backing catches up (typically ≥98% or debt nearly retired), dynamic tax turns off and the normal 0.1% transaction tax credits Reserve Gold again.
  • Voluntary burns — /pay reserve <amount> destroys Notes from your wallet (donation burn), lowering circulating supply.

Live backing ratio, debt retired, and supply checks: economy page. In-game: /tax for your effective rate.

Treasury grant policy

Automated functions and grant-type payouts always debit the Server Reserve — never direct wallet minting.

Always treasury-sourced

  • Vote rewards, playtime milestones
  • Staff /grant, loan disbursements, onboarding fee refunds
  • Discord link/activity/first-message payouts, public-reachout grant announcements
  • Council-approved treasury grants — linked players submit at /governance/grant/; weighted majority must hold 24 hours to pass and pay from reserve

Ended launch programs (Jul 2026)

  • Map-return grant (1000 G — historical rules)
  • /cmdtest command onboarding (up to 1000 G cap)

Not treasury grants

  • Player-to-player /pay, shop trades, contract escrow
  • Loan principal moving between borrower and reserve on repay
  • Mayor-set town/nation taxes (move to town/nation banks, not reserve)

Large treasury payouts that deliver physical gold follow Gold redemption (blocks first, then portable carriers when inventory is full).

Gold redemption

All Gold is redeemable. Wallet G converts to physical gold items at the fixed mint peg:

ItemValue
Gold nugget¹⁄₉ G (~0.11 G)
Gold ingot · raw gold1 G
Gold block9 G

Redeem with /mint gold <amount|max>. Deposit with /mint hand or /mint all. The wallet credit is the mint value minus the 0.1% transaction tax to the reserve — you are not charged extra on top of the gold you deposit.

Overflow & large payouts

When paying stacks of gold blocks and the player cannot fit more gold in inventory, the server uses portable gold carriers in order:

  1. Gold blocks (and ingots/nuggets) — standard mint peg items
  2. Satchels / handbags — compact redeemable gold storage
  3. Leather carriers — lowest-tier portable form when space is tight

Carriers hold redeemable Gold at the mint peg; minting them credits wallet G like physical gold. Prefer carriers over leaving wallet-only credit or dropping loose stacks on the ground for treasury grants.

Net worth

Net worth measures total wealth — not wallet balance alone.

net_worth ≈ wallet G + valued inventory + redeemable gold in player chests + shop stock + physical gold in storage (mint peg)

  • Physical gold items (blocks, ingots, nuggets, and approved carriers) are valued at the mint peg.
  • RootMC periodically scans player inventory, ender chests, shop chests, and loaded containers for physical gold in storage — reported on economy overview and reserve supply checks.
  • Gold stored in player-specific chests (and ender chest, inventory snapshots) counts toward net worth on sync.
  • Shop stock is valued at the blended buy-side reference — median in-stock sell average (what buyers pay) blended with recent price history — not each shop’s own listing price.
  • Town and nation bank balances are separate from player net worth on web leaderboards.

See leaderboard, player profiles, and /baltop players. Link with /rootmc link for public stats.

Transaction tax — 0.1% (dynamic when over-issued)

Withheld from the receiver on transfers and shop payouts. Customers and payers always pay the quoted or entered amount — never tax on top. When Notes are fully backed, the receiving side gets the amount minus 0.1% and the difference credits the Server Reserve.

Dynamic transaction tax

While circulating Notes exceed audited /mint backing, the effective rate rises toward the backing shortfall (capped by config). Withheld Gold burns unbacked Notes instead of crediting the reserve. When backing catches up (typically ≥98%), dynamic tax turns off and the normal 0.1% reserve credit returns. Check /tax in-game or economy overview for the live rate.

PvP death fee — 10% of balance

PvP only. 40% of fee → reserve, 60% → killer. Environmental/mob deaths: no fee.

Inactivity tax

Root-Upkeep at midnight HST. Grace: 7 days. Rates: week 1 0.01%, week 2 0.05%, week 3 0.1%, week 4+ 1.0% of current balance per day. Same tiers for idle town/nation banks when no member is active.

Personal loans

Default cap 100 G; with purchased ranks, cap equals that tier’s /rank buy price (up to 150,000 G). 10% interest; one active loan; disbursements from reserve.

Mayor-set taxes

Resident tax max 8%/day, 500 G/resident/day. Nation tax on towns max 10%, 800 G/town/day. Defaults 0%. Gold moves to town/nation banks — not the Server Reserve.

Shop pricing cap

Listings cannot exceed 110% of rolling market average. Not a tax — a price ceiling at create/edit time.

Conduct & fair play

  • No cheating — x-ray, dupes, unfair clients, or exploit abuse.
  • Honest trade — shop prices within the 110% cap; no scamming beyond disclosed shop terms.
  • Respect other players and staff decisions on reserve grants and governance.
  • Real-money token holdings do not purchase gameplay advantages — see token disclaimer.
